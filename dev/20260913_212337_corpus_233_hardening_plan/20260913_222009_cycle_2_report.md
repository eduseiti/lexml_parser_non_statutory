## 1. Summary

**Verdict: complete.**

Suite **6 052 passed / 0 failed / 4 skipped / 2 deselected**, up from 6 028.
Across the 233-document corpus, documents carrying a defective URN component
fall **46 → 23**, and the corpus now emits **233 distinct URNs with zero
collisions** — the identity collision §1.5 records is closed.

Four things are worth stating plainly.

**1. The plan undercounted, and the measurement is what found it.** §1.3 records
24 incomplete URNs. Measured over the emitted XML, the true figure is **46**:
§1.3 counted `Metadata.missing` only, and missed 27 documents that silently took
`authority="federal"` from the `generic` profile default. Those are Finding C
(§1.4) surfacing as a URN defect — and a URN naming the *wrong* issuer is worse
than one naming none, because nothing flags it. Escalated before implementation;
the user chose to fix all 46.

**2. Some URNs were confidently wrong, which the plan never anticipated.**
`nota_pgfn_crj_1114_2012` emitted `…:portaria:0000;294` — it had read *"Portaria
PGFN Nº 294/2010"*, a **citation of another act**, as its own identity. The root
cause was one character: `_EPIGRAPH_RE`'s type group forbade `/`, so
`NOTA PGFN/CRJ/Nº 1114/2012` did not match at paragraph 1 and the scan fell
through to paragraph 2. Four documents had that shape. This is the cycle's most
valuable finding, because these documents **did** state their identity and the
parser was looking past it — a fix strictly better than any filename fallback.

**3. A bug in my own new helper was caught by my own new test.**
`_number_from_filename` first accepted only a trailing `_YYYYMMDD`, so the four
`_YYYY`-shaped names returned `None` and the helper was near-inert for that
shape — the corpus numbers it appeared to recover were actually recovered by the
epigraph fix. `test_a_four_digit_year_is_not_a_number` failed and exposed it. Had
I written the test to match the implementation rather than the intent, the
helper would have shipped dead.

**4. Unexpected golden movement was escalated, not absorbed.** 14 of 15 metadata
goldens failed after the first change — far beyond the 2 the user had confirmed.
Rather than regenerate, I diffed field-by-field and proved the other 13 moved
*only* by gaining the additive `number_source` key, with **zero URN changes**.
Only then did I regenerate.

## 7. Deviations and open items

- **`solucao_consulta` siglas live on `generic` for now.** Building that profile
  is Cycle 3's deliverable, so the entries sit on `generic` and Cycle 3 should
  move them. Recorded as m-4.
- **Deliverable 4 is a statement, not a lock** — the plan's own "or an explicit
  statement" wording. `parse -o` is documented single-invocation; a lock correct
  across NFS and interrupted processes is not worth its complexity when the
  collision it guarded is now closed at the source.
- **`nota_pgfn_crj_*` documents still carry the wrong doc_type** (`portaria`,
  `sumula`) because no `nota` profile exists — the same gap A-2.4 records for
  `nota_tecnica`. Their *numbers* are now right. A `nota` profile is the fix, and
  it is not this cycle's.
- **The corpus is research data outside the repository.** 11 of the 20 new tests
  skip without it. That is the honest arrangement, but it does mean CI on a bare
  checkout exercises 9 of them.
