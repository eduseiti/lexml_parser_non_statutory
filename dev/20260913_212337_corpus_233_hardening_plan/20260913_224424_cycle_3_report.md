## 1. Summary

**Verdict: complete.**

Suite **6 082 passed / 0 failed / 4 skipped / 2 deselected**, up from 6 052.
Corpus flatness falls **115 → 86** overall and **58 → 33** for `sc_cosit`,
rules-only. **No golden file moved** and no sample's routing, segmentation or
measured shape moved.

Four things are worth stating plainly.

**1. The plan's premise for its main deliverable was wrong, and the measurement
found it.** Cycle 3 exists to close the flatness of the corpus's dominant genre,
and the plan proposed doing it with "genre-specific front-matter expectations" —
profile data. But the *Relatório / Fundamentos / Conclusão* skeleton is written
in **title case**, and `is_prose_form_header` requires an upper-case ratio of
0.85 (`Relatório` scores ~0.11). Those headings were never proposed as
candidates, so **no referee was ever asked about them** — they were invisible
under every configuration, refereed runs included. No amount of profile data or
referee budget could have closed this. `par_cosit_26`'s upper-case `RELATÓRIO`,
the paragraph A-H.1 was written for, is why the gate looked sufficient.

**2. The genre was not merely unprofiled — twelve documents were profiled as
something else.** Ten scored 0.40 on `jurisprudencia_generico`'s unanchored STJ
pattern (soluções *discussing* STJ case law), and two tied at 0.9 with `servico`
on a bare `carne-leao` pattern, winning on **registration order alone**. Those
two emitted a `:servico:` URN type — a solução de consulta claiming to be a
taxpayer service page. A tie decided by a list literal is exactly what
`test_winning_margin` exists to forbid, and §1.4 models none of this.

**3. An approved change was reverted on evidence, not absorbed.** The user
approved anchoring `jurisprudencia_generico`'s authority patterns (Q-3) as "the
same repair as Cycle 2's m-6". It was not: **unnecessary** (the new profile's
0.9 epigraph outranks 0.40 either way — measured both ways, 125/127 identical),
**insufficient** (two documents open a line with the court's name), and **not
free** — it moved `REsp_1306393`'s front/body boundary and its body confidence
0.0 → 0.3. Reverted, with the reasoning recorded in the code.

**4. That defect was invisible to every golden.** The tree stayed
`(0, 0, True, …)` — same sections, same depth, still flat — so all 11 golden
kinds were byte-identical. Only `test_measured_shape`, which asserts confidence
*independently of the goldens*, caught it. That test's own docstring claims this
is why it exists; this cycle is the first time it collected.

## 7. Deviations and open items

- **The `jurisprudencia_generico` anchoring the user approved was not done**,
  and the approved *goal* — twelve documents reclaimed — is met without it. The
  evidence is in §1.3, m-2 and A-3.2. If the unanchored pattern is wanted gone
  on principle, it needs a repair that does not move `REsp_1306393`, which is
  its own piece of work.
- **43 of the 125 get fewer than two declared headings inside their body span**,
  which is why flatness falls to 33 rather than near zero. The cause is
  **segmentation, not `section_res`**: `sc_cosit_100`'s body is truncated to
  blocks 1–26, so `Fundamentos` (42) and `Conclusão` (164) fall outside it
  entirely. These documents repeat their whole ementa block — a portal copy and
  a document copy — and the back-matter/annex finder reads the repetition as an
  ending. **This is the single largest remaining flatness cause in the genre**
  and it is a natural Cycle 1 input.
- **Two documents remain outside the profile** — `sc_15_20090309` and
  `sc_6007_20190325`, bare-`sc` regional soluções whose first line is a portal
  banner, not an epigraph. Already recorded as residue in Cycle 2's report §5.
- **`ad_pgfn` is untouched at 14/15 flat.** §1.2 calls it the strongest
  recogniser-gap signal; it is not this cycle's genre, and Cycle 1 owns it.

## 8. Next cycle readiness

Plan §3 puts **Cycle 1 — a truthful account of flatness** next, and §3's own
reasoning for placing it after Cycle 3 holds: the taxonomy should be designed
against what remains. It now inherits a sharper picture:

- flatness is **86/233**, not 115, and the genre that dominated it is handled;
- the largest remaining single cause is **named and localised** — body-span
  truncation from repeated front matter, 43 documents in this genre alone;
- `ad_pgfn` at 14/15 is now the clearest unexplained concentration;
- a **third** flatness cause exists that §1.2's proposed taxonomy does not
  model: *the structure was found but fell outside the body span*. That is
  neither "no candidate" nor "candidates rejected" nor "genuinely flat";
- `section_res` is available to any genre with a stereotyped skeleton, and
  `W_SECTION_DECLARED` / `profile_declared` make its use visible in telemetry;
- the rules-only baseline is established and reproducible offline, which is what
  Cycle 6 will measure the referee against.
