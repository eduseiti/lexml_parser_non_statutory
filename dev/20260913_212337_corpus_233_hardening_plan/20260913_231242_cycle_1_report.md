## 1. Summary

**Verdict: complete.**

Suite **6 151 passed / 0 failed / 4 skipped / 2 deselected**, up from 6 082.
Every flat tree now names why it is flat and records what fraction of the
document its body span covered. The 15 hierarchy goldens moved, exactly as
confirmed in advance, and **only** by gaining two keys — proved mechanically,
not asserted.

Four things are worth stating plainly.

**1. The plan's proposed taxonomy was wrong in two ways, and measuring is what
found it.** §1.2 proposes splitting flatness into *no candidate* / *candidates
rejected* / *a single continuous prose run*. The corpus has no "continuous prose
run" bucket at all; its fourth real cause is an **empty body span**, which the
plan does not model. And a "scores too weak" bucket — the natural fifth — is
**structurally unreachable**: `unify_levels` refuses a solitary label at its
`solitary`/`orphan` guards *before* `_score` is called, so across all 155 corpus
documents with assignments the lowest mean score is **0.7553**. Flatness from
scoring is always damping. Shipping that code would have invited consumers to
handle a case that cannot arise.

**2. "No candidate" mostly does not mean "unstructured", which changes what the
cause is worth on its own.** 41 of the 52 `no_candidate` documents have a body
span of ≤6 blocks; the bucket's median span coverage is **0.09**, and **82 of
all 86** flat documents have a span covering under half the document. Reporting
those 52 as "no structure found" would have replaced one silence with a more
confident one. `span_coverage` is what makes the cause legible, and it **cannot
be computed where the tree is built** — `build_tree` receives an already-sliced
span and never sees the blocks outside it.

**3. §1.2's "strongest recogniser-gap signal" is not a recogniser gap.**
`ad_pgfn` at 14/15 flat was the cycle's headline suspicion. It does not hold:
`is_prose_form_header` fires 18 times across the 22 `ad_pgfn`/`adn_cst`
documents and **every hit is a signature**; 21 of 22 documents reject nothing,
because nothing heading-shaped is ever proposed; and 19 have a body of 0–3
blocks against `MIN_SECTIONS_FOR_FULL_CONFIDENCE = 3`. The whole operative
content of one of these acts is a single quoted sentence. **Flat is correct**,
and no `section_res` is recommended for `ato_declaratorio`. A negative finding,
and the one the plan actually needed.

**4. My own first implementation was wrong, and the corpus measurement caught
it.** `_flat_cause` tested `not candidates`, but `collect_candidates` appends a
`Candidate` for *every* non-empty paragraph and lets the `is_candidate` property
decide — so the branch was dead and the first run produced `no_candidate 0 /
all_rejected 67` instead of 52/15. Because the expected partition came from an
independent measurement rather than from the implementation, the discrepancy was
visible immediately. Had I written the check to match my code, it would have
shipped mis-attributing 52 documents.

## 7. Deviations and open items

- **Two segmentation defects were found and left unfixed**, per the user's
  decision (Q-4) and the plan's own "Cycle 1 measures; Cycle 3 acts".
  `adn_cst_20_19890821` and `adn_cst_29_19860625` lose their operative `DECLARA`
  paragraph to the front-matter hull (`body = None`); `pn_cosit_1_20020924` has
  `body = None` despite **79 blocks** because three ementa headings were read as
  signatures. Conservation holds in all three, so no invariant fails and no test
  catches them — which is exactly why they are worth recording. All three would
  still be flat once repaired; the defect is *where the text lives*.
- **The 40-document declared-heading truncation is now measurable but not
  repaired.** `span_coverage` makes it visible; fixing it is segmentation work.
- **The 6 corpus-gated tests ran here rather than skipping**, because the
  research corpus is present in this environment (233 documents at the resolved
  path). Their skip path was then **verified directly** rather than assumed, by
  re-running the module against a synthetic repository root with no
  `br-taxqa-r_v2.0` sibling: `63 passed, 6 skipped`, each skip naming the absent
  path. So a bare checkout exercises 63 of the 69, and the 6 that need the
  corpus degrade the way `requires_nested` and `requires_linker` already do.
- **`test_golden_roundtrips` failed before regeneration**, for the ordinary
  reason that the committed files predated the keys. It is green after.
