### 2.2 Two corrections to the plan's implied taxonomy

**The "weak scores" bucket is structurally unreachable.** Across all **155**
documents that have assignments, the *minimum* mean score is **0.7553**; the
distinct values are all in the 0.75–0.90 band. Nothing anywhere is below 0.5.
The mechanism is `unify_levels`: a solitary label is `continue`d at the
`solitary`/`orphan` guards *before* `_score` is ever called, so `W_LABEL_SOLO`
(0.25) never reaches an assignment. Bucket (c) is therefore **entirely
damping** — all 8 documents have exactly `n_assign == 1`, confidence
0.2667–0.30, which is `mean × 1/3`.

Verified directly: `document_confidence([0.85])` → 0.2833,
`document_confidence([0.85, 0.85])` → 0.5667, `document_confidence([0.85]*3)`
→ 0.85, with `CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD = 0.5` and
`MIN_SECTIONS_FOR_FULL_CONFIDENCE = 3`.

**"No candidate" mostly does not mean "unstructured".** 41 of the 52 have a
body span of ≤6 blocks; median span coverage across the bucket is **0.09**.
Reporting these as "genuinely unstructured" to a consumer would be misleading —
the span was too small to *contain* a candidate.

### 2.3 Body-span truncation

- **82 of 86** flat documents have a body span covering <50% of the document's
  blocks; median coverage **0.089**.
- **40** flat documents have ≥2 profile-declared headings in the whole document
  but <2 inside the body span — by cause: 28 `no_candidate`, 8 `all_rejected`,
  4 `too_few_sections`.
- `sc_cosit_100_20200928`, confirmed: body span 1–26 of **186** blocks,
  declared headings at blocks 22, 42, 164 — only block 22 inside.
- `solucao_consulta`: **45 of 128** by profile have <2 declared headings in the
  body span. Cycle 3's report §7 said "43 of 125"; **neither figure
  reproduces** — there is no grouping yielding 125 or 43. 42 of those 45 are
  flat, and all 42 flat `solucao_consulta` documents have <2 declared headings
  in body — a near-perfect predictor.

**Placement consequence (decisive):** this is **not computable inside
`build_tree`**. `build_tree` receives only the already-sliced span blocks plus a
`Span` carrying `start`/`end`; blocks 42 and 164 are never passed in, and the
`Span` does not know the document's total block count. It **must** be computed
in `infer_hierarchy`, which holds both `doc.blocks` and `segmentation.body`.

  `tree.signals == DocSignals()` *against freshly-constructed defaults* with a

## 4. Expanded goals

### D-1 — the cause taxonomy (plan deliverable 1)

`hierarchy/evidence.py`:

```python
FLAT_CAUSES: tuple[str, ...] = (
    "no_candidate",       # nothing heading-shaped was proposed
    "all_rejected",       # candidates were found and every one was refused
    "too_few_sections",   # assigned, but too few to be a shape (damping)
    "empty_body_span",    # the body span contained no blocks
)
```

A closed vocabulary, for the reason `WARNING_CODES` and `BLOCKER_CODES` are
closed: a diagnostic a caller cannot enumerate is one a caller cannot act on.

`DocSignals` gains two fields, both with inert defaults:

```python
flat_cause: str = ""        # one of FLAT_CAUSES, or "" when not flat
span_coverage: float = 0.0  # body blocks / document blocks, 0.0 when unknown
```

Both are serialized by `to_dict()` / restored by `from_dict()`.

### D-2 — where each is computed

- **`flat_cause`** in `build_tree`, where the evidence lives. It is a pure
  function of what that function already has: `blocks`, `candidates`,
  `assignments`, `flat`.
- **`span_coverage`** in `infer_hierarchy` (§2.3 — `build_tree` *cannot* see
  it), applied to the body tree only. Annexes keep 0.0: an annex's coverage
  against the whole document is not a meaningful ratio.

### D-3 — surfacing (plan deliverable 1)

| Surface | Change |
|---|---|
| `parse --format=json` | `flat_cause`, `span_coverage` beside the existing `flat` / `hierarchy_confidence` |
| `corpus --format=json` | same two on each `DocumentOutcome`, plus `by_flat_cause` and `by_genre_flatness` on the report |
| `corpus` text report | a flat-cause tally and a per-genre flatness section |
| `dump-tree --why` | the cause and coverage on the tree summary line |

### D-4 — per-genre flatness (plan deliverable 2)

`corpus.py` gains `genre_of(source)` and, on `CorpusReport`,
`by_genre_flatness()` returning `(genre, total, flat)` triples sorted flat-count
descending then name — `_ranked`'s determinism rule (invariant #4).
`DocumentOutcome` gains `genre`, `flat_cause`, `span_coverage`.

### D-5 — the investigation (plan deliverable 3)

Written to `docs/`, **not acted on**, per the plan and Q-4.

## 8. Risks

- **The goldens move, and that is the thing Cycle 3's criterion was written to
  catch.** Mitigation: the diff must be *additive keys only*. I verify
  key-by-key that no `confidence`, `flat`, `sections` or `signals` value moved,
  the way Cycle 2 proved its 13 unintended metadata goldens moved only by
  gaining `number_source`. `test_measured_shape` is the independent check.
- **A cause computation that perturbs inference.** The cause is derived from
  values `build_tree` already computed; nothing feeds back. Pinned by
  `test_measured_shape` and the conservation invariants.
- **`span_coverage` on a `None` body span.** `infer_hierarchy` must handle
  `segmentation.body is None` (11 corpus documents, and samples `ad_srf_22` /
  `adn_cosit_19`) without dividing by zero.
- **Scope creep into Cycle 3 territory.** Q-4 settled it: report only. The
  temptation is real — 40 documents would gain structure from a segmentation
  fix — and it is explicitly not this cycle's.
