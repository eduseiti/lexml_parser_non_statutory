## 1. Summary

All 15 samples now ingest into a typed, rendering-agnostic `StyledDoc`, losslessly and deterministically, with goldens committed and regenerable by one documented command. `pytest` reports **373 passed, 0 failed** — Cycle 0's 107 unchanged plus **266 new**.

The cycle's most valuable output is not the reader but a **correction to the plan's central quotation-detection claim**. Plan §8 asserted that `parecer_93`'s 21 quoted articles show `ind≈2880–2930` against a *modal 0*. Measured, the count and band are exactly right — and the baseline is wrong in a way that would have broken Cycle 4. The modal direct indent **is 2908, the quote band itself**: 137 non-empty paragraphs sit in that band, because every inciso, alínea and continuation line of a quoted article inherits the indent of the `Art.` line heading it. Only 37 paragraphs occupy the small band. A Cycle 4 rule thresholding "indent ≫ modal ⇒ quotation" would therefore have classified **most of the document as unquoted body** — silently, which §10 lists as the highest-severity risk in the project ("quoted statute misread as articulation — high, silent corruption"). Recorded as Amendment A-1.1, with the corrected discriminator (quote band vs small band, disjoint by an order of magnitude) and a test that pins it.

### For Cycle 4 specifically

The quotation guard must read **quote band vs small band**, not quote band vs modal. Concretely, in `parecer_93`: 137 non-empty paragraphs at 2880–2930, 37 at 7–60, 24 scattered between, and the two populations are disjoint by an order of magnitude — any threshold in 100–2800 separates them. Only 20 of the 137 in-band paragraphs begin with `Art.`, so indentation identifies the *region* and the label grammar must find article boundaries within it. One quoted article (`Art. 4º`, at 240) sits outside the band entirely, which is why §2.5's pairing with citation antecedent and monotonicity is not optional.
