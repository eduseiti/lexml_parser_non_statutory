## 1. Summary

**Verdict: complete.**

The statutory emitter is delivered. `port_mf_277` — the one sample in fifteen
that §4.4 routes to `norma` — renders as `ParteInicial`/`Articulacao`/
`ParteFinal` with its `ANEXO ÚNICO` split off as a sibling `!anexo1` document,
and **both documents validate against both shipped schemas**. Primary and annex
together carry every source word exactly once. The other fourteen samples are
refused the route, which §4.4 calls this route's actual job.

§4.2's validate-then-fallback is implemented as a real gate on **three**
conditions rather than one, and it earned that on its first run: the cycle's own
first statutory render of `port_mf_277` was **valid on both schemas and 29 words
short**. A validity-only gate would have published it. The defect was in
extraction, not in the emitter (A-6.4).

Five findings shaped the work, four of them contradicting the plan, all measured
against both schemas before a line was written:

- **A-6.1** — `lexml09-flexivel` constrains `idArtigo` with an `xsd:pattern`.
  `art1`/`art1_cpt` is **schema-mandated**, not stylistic, and `pp1_art1` is
  rejected — so Cycle 5's path-composed allocator cannot issue dispositivo ids.
- **A-6.2** — `ParteInicial` and `ParteFinal` are **closed sequences**. A-5.1's
  "regions, not parts" rendering, which exists because 40 non-empty blocks in 6
  samples sit inside a hull and inside no named part, has no statutory
  equivalent. Front residue folds into `Preambulo`; back residue has nowhere to
  go and falls back.
- **A-6.3** — the gate is validity **and** conservation **and** coverage.
- **A-6.4** — `leaf_texts` never read `Epigrafe`, `Ementa`, `NomePessoa` or
  `Cargo`, and double-counted a `Caput`'s echoed `Rotulo`.
- **A-6.5** — the §2.9 annex convention existed in **two** copies; a third was
  refused and all three folded into one shared module.

### The measured content model

33 probe cases against both shipped schemas, plus `lexml-proposed/`, **before**
the emitter was written.

Confirmed **valid**: §4.3's primary verbatim; `Articulacao` alone, without
either `Parte`; `Caput` *without* its own `Rotulo` (so D-3's duplication is
convention, not necessity); `Paragrafo`; `Inciso` inside `Caput`; a flat
`AgrupamentoHierarquico` inside `Articulacao`; a `Caput` with no `<p>`; ids
`art1u` and `art1_par1u`; `Preambulo` carrying several `<p>` (which is what
makes A-6.2's front-residue fold possible); `Assinaturas` as a wrapper;
`Anexo/DocumentoArticulado`; a nested annex body on `lexml-proposed/`.

Confirmed **invalid**: `pp1_art1` as an `Artigo` id (**A-6.1**); an `Artigo`
without a `Rotulo`; a `Caput` before its `Rotulo`; an empty `Articulacao`;
`Anexos` before `ParteFinal`; `Agrupamento`, `p` or `DispositivoGenerico`
directly in `Articulacao`; `Agrupamento` or bare `p` inside `ParteInicial` or
`ParteFinal` (**A-6.2**); an extra `<p>` inside `Assinatura`; a childless
`AgrupamentoHierarquico`; a `table` or an `ol` inside a `Caput` (D-6); a
`Preambulo` without an `id`; `Anexo/Norma`; a nested annex body on `lexml/`.

### Fan-out

Two subagents, disjoint file ownership, **neither permitted to write `src/`,
`scripts/`, `tests/golden/` or `tests/conftest.py`** — the Cycle 2 incident's
standing rule. Each was briefed with the spec path, verbatim signatures, and
"report a required major change, do not make it".

| Party | Owned | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| me | all of `src/`, `scripts/`, `tests/golden/`, the plan and cycle documents | both emitters, the goldens, the golden tests, every production fix |
| subagent A | `tests/unit/test_render_norma.py` | 33 tests; **found two real defects in my code** and reported rather than fixed them |
| subagent B | `tests/unit/test_render_anexo.py`, `tests/regression/test_norma_conservation.py` | 35 tests; 12 anti-vacuity mutations, 12 caught |

Subagent A wrote **12 tests beyond the spec's table**, three of them forced by
its own surviving mutants (see §3), and one — pinning that a `Caput`'s echoed
rótulo is excluded from extraction *exactly once* — came from subagent B on the
same reasoning: an exclusion that is never asserted is an exclusion the next
change can silently remove.

**Subagent A found two defects, and the way it reported them is worth
recording.** Forbidden from touching `src/`, it marked both
`@pytest.mark.xfail(strict=True)` with a diagnosis in the reason string —
so the gap could not quietly become the specification, and the fix could not
land unnoticed (a strict xfail fails as XPASS the moment the bug is fixed).
Both were real:

1. **`Parágrafo único` never got its `art1_par1u` id.** My detection read
   `"unic" in text.lower()`, and `único` is *accented* — which is how every
   real Brazilian legal text writes it. The accented form silently became
   `art1_par1`, an id asserting a numbered `§ 1º` the document never wrote.
   Fixed with Cycle 4's own accent-folding `fold()`; both spellings now work.
2. **`ARTIGO_ID_RE` was wider than the XSD.** Its three groups were
   independently optional, so it accepted `art1_inc1` — an inciso hanging off
   an article rather than off a caput or parágrafo, which both schemas reject.
   Fixed by nesting the `_inc` group inside `_cpt|_par`.

**Verified, not trusted.** `git diff --stat src/ scripts/ tests/golden/` shows
only my own files. I re-ran the full suite, the golden regeneration, the
capability probe and the fallback paths myself, and independently confirmed no
mutation residue after both sweeps (subagent B's 12 and my own 5).

I also found and closed a third defect myself: a **dead `unico` branch** in the
artigo path. Cycle 4's `ARTICLE_RE` requires a digit, so `Art. único` parses as
`None` and never reaches it. Rather than leave unreachable code, the branch was
removed and the measured reason recorded in a comment — inventing a number the
grammar refused to read is the fabrication A-R.6 withdrew a whole cycle over.

## 5. Changes to existing features

Five, in [`…_cycle_6_changes.md`](20260829_155837_cycle_6_changes.md).
**No delivered behaviour changed** — all 32 `generico` and `generico-aninhado`
goldens are byte-identical, and every pre-existing golden kind reports
`0 changed`.

**One major change, escalated and approved: C-2**, `leaf_texts` reading the four
statutory elements and skipping a `Caput`'s echoed `Rotulo`. Three options were
put to the user (skip the copy / stop emitting it / let every statutory render
fail conservation); the user chose skipping the copy, which keeps §4.3's shape
and the reference parser's convention intact. The precedent is exact and already
in that module: `Bloco nome="nivel"` is excluded for the same stated reason.

Six changes were considered and **rejected**, two of them structural: teaching
`render/ids.py` the dispositivo pattern (two incompatible id grammars in one
allocator would make Rule A's "structural rather than aspirational" guarantee
conditional on which method a caller used), and selecting the annex's nested
form from the capability probe (output varying with the checkout cannot have
goldens).

## 7. Deviations and open items

- **`Alinea` and `Item` are not built.** No corpus document reaches them, so no
  test could discharge them — A-2.4's reasoning. A document needing them falls
  back to `generico`, which loses no text. Blocks nothing.
- **`Art. único` is not reachable from a document, and one spec row was
  corrected to say so.** Cycle 4's `ARTICLE_RE` requires a digit, so `Art.
  único` parses as `None` and becomes caput prose of the article above.
  `DispositivoIds.artigo(unico=True)` exists, is schema-valid and is tested, but
  only a caller building an articulation directly reaches it. Left alone
  deliberately: numbering an article the grammar refused to read is fabrication.
  The spec's row for this test originally read "synthetic `Art. único` →
  `art1u`", an **end-to-end claim the pipeline cannot make**; it was corrected
  in place, with the reason, after both the implementation and subagent A
  independently hit the same wall. `Parágrafo único` *does* run end to end.
  **If a real `Art. único` appears in the 300-document corpus, this is where it
  will be noticed** — the document will fall back rather than mis-number.
- **A body with nested sections, a table or a list cannot be articulated.**
  Both schemas reject a `table` or `ol` inside a `Caput`, so such a document
  falls back rather than dropping the node (D-6). Correct, and worth revisiting
  only if the wider corpus shows it is common.
- **One norma sample is thin evidence.** Mitigated by synthetic fixtures for
  every path the corpus cannot reach and by 17 mutations, but it remains the
  cycle's main risk and Cycle 9's scale-out is where it gets tested.

## 8. Next cycle readiness

**Cycle 7 — Segmentation output** inherits:

- **Three emitters, three goldens.** `segment_norma.xsl` now has a committed
  artifact to run against, and the oracle A-R.5 makes three-way has its third
  input.
- **A second id grammar in the corpus.** The round-trip reader relocated here

  `test_golden_ids_unique` asserts across the bundle rather than assuming.

  reader need not re-infer the route — which matters precisely because §4.2's
  fallback means a `norma`-routed document may be a `generico` artifact.
- **A-6.4's extraction rule is load-bearing for segmentation.** A `Caput`'s
  `Rotulo` is skipped and the four statutory elements are read; a segmenter
  that re-derived text by its own rules would disagree with the conservation
  invariant this cycle just established.
