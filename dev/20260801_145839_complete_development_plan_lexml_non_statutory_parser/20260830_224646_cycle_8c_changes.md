## MAJOR-1 — `own_articulation`'s prompt context widened (A-Q.7)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **What changed** | `routing/viability.py` · `_adjudicate_articles` |
| **Before → after** | `ctx` was the immediately preceding non-empty paragraph. It is now the **nearest preceding citation antecedent** — the nearest earlier paragraph for which `names_external_norm` is true — falling back to the immediately preceding paragraph when there is none |
| **Why** | The investigation record's §2.3. On `par_cosit_26` the referee overrode p#47 and p#53 **wrongly**, at confidence 0.95 and 0.70, reasoning correctly about the statute and drawing the wrong conclusion about *whose* it is — because the sentence naming the owner (`Lei nº 7.713, de 1988 - "Art. 1º-…`, block 45) sat two paragraphs back, structurally outside `MAX_CONTEXT_CHARS`' single-paragraph window |
| **Blast radius** | Every `own_articulation` cache key moves. Two of the four committed fixtures were re-keyed (`p#47`: `1a2ea8e5…` → `04f000a5…`; `p#53`: `6d17981b…` → `b53aaec5…`); `p#46` and `parecer_93` `p#36` were **already** being handed their antecedent and did not move. Verdicts, confidences, rationales and the hand-authored `origin` are untouched, and the new keys were **derived by replaying the corpus, never typed** (the A-C.4 method). No rule verdict changes, so **no golden changes** from A-Q.7 alone |
| **Plan impact** | §7.3's `MAX_CONTEXT_CHARS` note; recorded as amendment **A-Q.7** |

## Minor changes

### MIN-1 — `QuotationAnalysis` gains two fields (A-Q.1)

`hierarchy/quotation.py`. `runs: tuple[QuoteRun, ...]` and
`rejected_heads: tuple[int, ...]`, both defaulting to empty. **`quoted` keeps
its exact meaning and its exact value**, so every existing consumer is
untouched by construction. `to_dict` gains two keys; `QuoteRun` is
golden-serialisable in the house style. Additive.

### MIN-2 — a quotation head is convicted as quoted material (A-Q.2)

`hierarchy/quotation.py` · `_convict_quotation_heads`. A paragraph opening with
a norm designation **and** a following article marker, immediately above
already-quoted material, is now quoted itself.

**This is the one behaviour change that moves a golden.** `par_cosit_26`
block 45 gains `class="quote"`; it was rendering as a bare `<p>` in a wall of
`class="quote"`. One sample, one paragraph, three golden files
(`hierarchy`, `generico`, `generico_aninhado`). Every other sample is
byte-identical.

### MIN-3 — `SECTION_KINDS` gains `citacao` (A-Q.4)

`model/nodes.py`. One new member. `Agrupamento/@nome` is an open `xsd:string`
(`lexml-base.xsd:241`), so this needs **no schema work** — verified by
validating all 15 samples with nesting active against both shipped schemas.

### MIN-4 — the `Referee` protocol gains a fourth method (A-Q.3)

`referee/protocol.py`, `prompts.py`, `adjudicate.py`, and all three
implementations (`null`, `api`, `local`). `quotation_boundary(excerpt, ctx)`
over the closed vocabulary `("boundary", "continuation")`.

**Test doubles had to grow the method**: `StubReferee`
(`tests/unit/test_referee_protocol.py`) and `Recorder`
(`tests/unit/test_cli.py`). Neither assertion was weakened — a protocol with a
new method simply has a new method.

### MIN-5 — `adjudicate` now enforces the closed vocabulary

`referee/adjudicate.py`. An answer outside `VOCABULARIES[kind]` becomes an
**abstention** rather than being recorded as a verdict.

Found by this cycle's own tests, and it is a real gap rather than a
convenience: `api.py` and `local.py` each validated the answer *they parsed*,
which covers every referee that speaks over a wire — but an **in-process**
referee reaches `adjudicate` directly and nothing checked it. It could never
produce a wrong *outcome* (a verdict outside the vocabulary matches no branch
any caller tests), but it was counted and reported as though the referee had
said something. `AlwaysOwnReferee`, the A-4b.6 adversary, was answering `"own"`
to boundary questions and having it recorded as an override.

### MIN-6 — `build_tree`, `infer_hierarchy` and `build_model` thread a referee

`hierarchy/tree.py`, `hierarchy/__init__.py`, `model/document.py`. Four new
keyword-only parameters (`doc_name`, `referee`, `log`, `logger`), all
defaulting such that **the no-referee path is byte-identical to before**. This
is the first place in the plan where a referee reaches hierarchy inference;
until now it could only change the *route* (record §2.2).

### MIN-7 — `assess_viability` passes its referee and log to the hierarchy it infers

`routing/viability.py`. When not handed a hierarchy it builds one, and was
building it with neither. Decisions raised inside `build_tree` were therefore
made and dropped, so `--decisions-report` under-counted by exactly the
boundaries — and **only** when the caller had not built the hierarchy first.
`build_model` always supplies one, so the real pipeline was never affected.

### MIN-8 — the flagged-decision census moves from 4 to 7

`tests/unit/test_telemetry.py`, `tests/unit/test_referee_api.py`,
`tests/regression/test_cli_corpus.py`. A-4b.3's "the corpus flags exactly four
decisions" becomes **seven**: four `own_articulation` plus three
`quotation_boundary`. `CORPUS_TOTAL` 47 → 50.

This is the threshold-drift alarm doing precisely its job, and it was **not**
loosened to accommodate the cycle: the counts are now pinned **per kind**, so a
drift in either still trips it, and `FLAGGED_DECISIONS` still names every
locator. The three additions are flagged *by design* —
`BOUNDARY_RULE_CONFIDENCE` (0.55) sits deliberately below `FLAG_THRESHOLD`
(0.60) so that no candidate can become structure without a referee.

The three boundary fixtures **override** their rule verdict, and one test's
per-record assertions were made kind-aware to say so rather than relaxed: the
rule verdict for a candidate is `continuation` (stay flat), so confirming a
boundary *is* an override. `report.check()` still reconciles.

## Considered and deliberately rejected

**Splitting a `ListNode` or `Table` to make a run boundary fall where we want
it.** `_run_of` returns `None` for a node straddling two runs, and A-Q.5's gate
then refuses the whole section. Cutting content open to produce a tidier
hierarchy would be inventing structure *inside* content, which is a worse
failure than staying flat.

**Letting the referee propose boundaries.** The record's §6 resolution, kept:
the referee may only confirm or veto a candidate the deterministic generator
already found. A wrong `class="quote"` is a mislabelling; a wrong nested
`Agrupamento` is a fabricated citable unit with its own URN.
