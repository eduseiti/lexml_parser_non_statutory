## 1. Summary

**Verdict: complete.**

A paragraph that is a section header **by meaning alone** can now open a
section, confirmed one at a time by an LLM referee that can only ever veto or
confirm — never volunteer. `par_cosit_26`'s `RELATÓRIO` and `CONCLUSÃO` become
top-level `AgrupamentoHierarquico`s holding the numbered items, which is the
shape the user reported as correct; the same document's six folio stamps,
letterhead block, form-field label and signatory cargo are proposed by the
generator and correctly refused.

The corpus surface is **31 candidates across 5 samples**, of which **6** are
confirmed. With `--referee=none` — what §9.3 pins for the whole suite — the
generator is not run at all and **all 135 goldens are byte-identical**.

## 5. Defects found in this cycle's own code

Full detail in the changes file. In short:

- **D-1 — a confirmed prose header silently disabled A-4.3's demotion.**
  `demote_numbered_containers` collects a run of same-depth *styled*
  assignments; a prose header has `style is None` and **split the run in half**,
  so neither half met `MIN_DEMOTION_RUN`. On `sumula_stj_125` this turned 7
  cases × 4 subsections into **48 top-level sections**. Reachable with the real
  fixtures, not only under attack — that document was one confirmed candidate
  away from it.
- **D-2 — a confirmed header could outrank a styled one.** The first
  implementation opened every header at depth 1, putting it above the author's
  own declared structure. Fixed by anchoring beneath the innermost open styled
  section; depth 1 is reached only when Word declares nothing, which is the
  `par_cosit_26` case.
- **D-3 — 12 fixtures recorded truncated context**, so their keys could not be
  re-derived from their own contents. Rewritten, every key re-verified by replay.

Both D-1 and D-2 were found by `test_an_adversarial_referee_does_not_move_sumula_stj_125`
on its first run — the test written to catch precisely this. The tempting
reading was that the test was over-strict; it was not, and §"Changes considered
and rejected" in the changes file records why relaxing it was refused.

## 7. Deviations and open items

**The spec's census was wrong, and the implementation refuted it.** The spec
said *33 candidates across 7 samples*, counting a signatory name each in
`ad_srf_22` and `adn_cosit_19`. Both have `Segmentation.body is None` — they
are entirely front and back matter (A-3.5) — so `build_tree` never sees those
paragraphs. **31 across 5** is the real surface. The spec and plan §17 were
corrected during implementation, and the discrepancy is pinned as
`test_documents_with_no_body_propose_nothing`.

**E-4 was met in a sharper form than written.** "`sumula_stj_125`'s tree
unchanged" turned out to be the wrong assertion: block 371's
`VOTO VENCIDO EM PARTE` is a genuine section in an unstyled tail, and confirming
it is correct. The test asserts the claim that actually matters — the styled
tree is untouched and the addition lands *inside* it, never beside it.

**`sumula_carf_42` stays flat, deliberately.** Its one confirmed header scores
0.8, which `document_confidence` damps to 0.267 (one section, threshold 3),
below `CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD`. A document does not have a structure on the
strength of one heading, and a referee gets no exemption from the rule that has
always refused a lone rótulo. Pinned as
`test_a_lone_confirmed_header_does_not_make_a_document_structured`.

**Deliberately not done:**

- **The running-header/footer artifact work** (`docs/20260830_205825_…`) — the
  user's answer to Q-4. The ~13 `Fl. n` and footer paragraphs in `par_cosit_26`
  are proposed and correctly refused, so this is a cost question, not a
  correctness one. Doing it later removes ~13 questions from the corpus.
- **Any change to the `own_articulation`, `section_kind` or
  `quotation_boundary` questions.** Their prompts and cache keys are untouched.

**Still open, unchanged:** whether the 31 verdicts generalise to the 300+ unseen
documents. The argument that the *negative classes* generalise even though the
15 samples do not is a design argument, not a measurement — **Cycle 9's corpus
scale-out is the thing that will answer it.** Also unchanged: LLM-doc §8
questions 1, 3, 4 (P-5, recording live fixtures) and 5.
