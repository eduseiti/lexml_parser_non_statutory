## 1. Summary

Every sample now carries a routing verdict, a referee that may adjudicate it,
and a decision log that counts what happened. `pytest` reports **3135 passed, 0
failed**: Cycles 0–4's 2462 unchanged plus **673 new**.

The cycle's central result is that **the discriminator is one number, and the
corpus hands it over without tuning**. Cycle 4's quotation guard had already
convicted every quoted article; subtracting those from the article census
leaves `articles_own`, and across all fifteen samples exactly one document has
a non-zero value: `port_mf_277`, with two, monotonic, covering 100% of its body
once Cycle 3's annex split is applied. §4.4's table falls out of that column
alone. The four gates, the genre priors and the blockers exist not to *find*
that document but to keep the other fourteen out — which is what §4.4 says the
job is: "statutory detection's main job is refusing false positives".

The second result is about the referee, and it is smaller than the plan
expected in a good way. Attributing per-paragraph confidence from Cycle 4's own
cues, the whole 15-document corpus flags **four** decisions — three in
`par_cosit_26` (plan §2.6's residual case, which has no indent band at all) and
**one** in `parecer_93`, whose 415 paragraphs and 25 quoted articles are carried
almost entirely by its declared quote band. A frontier API is warranted for
four questions per fifteen documents, and the recorded fixtures make those four
answerable offline forever.

The third is a warning, and it is the same warning Cycle 4 issued. A mutation
sweep over this cycle's twenty-seven decision points found three survivors, and
**all three were places the corpus cannot reach**: two `quotation_confidence`
branches no sample exercises, and one adjudication guard the shipped thresholds
make unreachable. The most serious defect of the cycle was found this way —
`DecisionRecord.agreed` counted a referee that *disagreed and was refused* as
having agreed, which is the specific error that manufactures evidence for
consulting the referee less. All are fixed and covered synthetically.

### One change was considered and **rejected as major**

**Reporting `agreed + overrode == flagged` as §7.4 writes it.** It is false
under `--referee=none`, the setting §9.3 pins for the whole regression suite.
Forcing it — by counting an unconsulted flagged decision as an agreement —
would report the rules as *confirmed* by a referee that was never asked.
Weakening the test was rejected under the cycle process. The plan was amended
instead (A-4b.4), and the original identity is asserted in exactly its original
form under an active referee, where it does apply.

### Three defects in this cycle's **own** code, found and fixed here

Not changes to delivered features — all three are in modules first written by
this cycle — but the way they were found is worth recording. Two were reported
by a test-authoring subagent under the brief's "report, do not fix" rule; the
third was found by the mutation sweep. All were reproduced before being acted
on.

| # | Defect | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-1 | `LocalReferee`'s JSON salvage was `r"\{.*\}"` with `DOTALL` — greedy, spanning first brace to last, not "the first balanced object" as its own comment claimed | A model that answers then repeats itself, or writes a brace-bearing preamble, becomes an **abstention**. Fail-safe intact, usefulness silently gone, and `--decisions-report` blames the network | Balanced-brace scan yielding every candidate, taking the first that parses; quotes and escapes tracked so a brace inside a rationale does not truncate it |
| D-2 | `LocalReferee`'s cache key used `model_path.name`, against a docstring promising the path | `models/q4/x.gguf` and `models/q8/x.gguf` — the ordinary quantisation layout — serve each other's verdicts, **with `cache_hit` recorded to say the answer is trustworthy** | Key on the full path |
| D-3 | `DecisionRecord.agreed` meant *consulted, not abstained, not overridden* | **The serious one.** A referee that answers, *contradicts* the rule, and is refused the override — reachable with the shipped constants whenever the referee is itself unsure — was counted as having **agreed**. §7.4 reads that number as "the rules were right but unsure" and prescribes tightening the thresholds when it is high. The bug manufactures precisely the evidence for consulting the referee *less*, out of the cases where it disagreed | `agreed` redefined as *answered and said what the rule said*; new `overruled` property and fourth report bucket; identity corrected to `agreed + overrode + overruled + abstained == consulted` |

Two tests written earlier in this same cycle pinned D-3's wrong definition and
were updated, with the history kept in their docstrings. No prior-cycle test was
touched.

### Two branches the corpus cannot reach

The sweep showed `quotation_confidence` has 8 branches and the corpus exercises
