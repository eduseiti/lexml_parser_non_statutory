## 1. Summary

Every sample's body — and, per the decision taken with the user, every annex —
now carries an inferred recursive tree. `pytest` reports **2462 passed, 0
failed**: Cycles 0–3's 1681 unchanged plus **781 new**.

The cycle's central result is that the plan's proposed quotation discriminator
is right in idea and wrong in arithmetic, and that measuring it properly is
what makes the whole cycle safe. Plan §2.5 proposed comparing each paragraph's
indent against the document's modal body indent. On `parecer_93` — the sample
the rule exists for — the quote band is **2908** and the modal body indent is
**2909**, one twip *above* it. The signal is real but it is not distance: body
text **inherits** 2909 from the `Normal` style, while every one of the 25
quoted articles **declares** its own indent. Band detection therefore has two
rules and picks whichever the document supports (amendment A-4.1), and a
corollary falls out of the same measurement: a paragraph Word itself declares a
heading is never quoted — without which `sumula_stj_125`'s seven centred
`EMENTA` headings land in the deviation band and the document loses a level of
structure.

The second result is that the plan's regression-critical requirement — *all the
indented `Art.` in `parecer_93` are content, not structure* — is best
discharged **structurally rather than by a rule**. On the generic route an
article is prose; articulation is the `norma` route's shape, which Cycles 4b
and 6 own. So `labels.py` recognises `Art.`/`§` for Cycle 4b's census and
`tree.py` never builds a `Section` from one (decision D-3). The property now
holds however the confidence arithmetic is later retuned, and it is asserted
across all 15 samples rather than one.

The third is a warning about what the corpus can and cannot test. Three defects
in this cycle's own code were found by its own tests, and the most serious —
`_list_node` silently dropping every nested list item — is **unreachable from
the corpus**: no sample has a contiguous multi-level Word list (A-4.6). It was
caught only because the spec's test plan called for a synthetic fixture, which
is the A-1.3 precedent doing its job a second time.

### The agents found three real defects, and all three were fixed here

Subagent reports are claims, and each of these was reproduced before acting on
it. Two came back as *findings reported, not fixed* — the correct behaviour
under the brief's prohibition — and one as a strict `xfail` pinning the gap so
that fixing it would fail loudly rather than silently pass.

| # | Defect | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `parse_label("2.1 -")` returned `raw="2.1"` with the hyphen stranded in the remainder — `_NUMERIC_RE` requires trailing whitespace and backtracked past it | A heading whose title sits on the next line would carry a body paragraph reading `-` | The whole-paragraph pattern is tried first |
| 2 | `names_external_norm` carried the noun `constituicao` but not the adjective `constitucional(is)`, so **plan §2.6's own worked antecedent** (`parecer_93` block 342) was not recognised | Nil today — `parecer_93`'s declared band already convicts the excerpt — but the citation antecedent is the cue that must carry indentation-free documents, the shape most of the 285 unseen ones are expected to take | `_NORM_WORDS` gains both adjective forms |
| 3 | `_list_node` dropped every nested list item | Silent text loss on any multi-level Word list | Rewritten recursively; clamps rather than drops in all three malformed shapes |

Two further gaps the agents pinned were closed while the fix was cheap and the
corpus could confirm nothing moved: a bare ordinal (`1º` alone on a line) parsed
as nothing at all, because every other kind had an end-of-string twin and the
ordinal had none; and `_PARAGRAFO_RE` did not accept the `-A` suffix that
`ARTICLE_RE` already did. Neither shape occurs in the corpus — no golden
changed — and both are written for the unseen documents, where a silently
unparsed heading would never be noticed. A lone ordinal still cannot become a
section: `ordinal` joined the singleton-sequence rejection.

In each case the test that pinned the defect was rewritten to assert the fixed
behaviour, with the history kept in its docstring.

### Four spec expectations were corrected by measurement

Recorded in the spec itself rather than quietly edited, following Cycle 3's
"second signer" precedent. The most consequential is **C-1**: the spec predicted
`parecer_93` would yield **0 sections and go flat**, and it yields **3** —
`IV -`, `V - CÁLCULO DO BENEFÍCIO ESPECIAL`, `VI - CONCLUSÃO`. Those are the
parecer's own roman-numbered chapters; `I`–`III` are lost only because the scan
mangled `III -` into `111 -`. Recovering three real chapters is the right
answer, not fabrication, and **the regression-critical requirement is
unaffected** — zero of the 25 `Art.` become structure. The flat-fallback test
moved to the five samples that genuinely produce nothing. C-2 (7 case sections,
not 8), C-3 (65 annex sections, not 130) and C-4 (no corpus multi-level list)
were miscounts corrected by measurement.

## 8. Next cycle readiness

**Cycle 4b — Statutory Viability Analyzer + LLM Referee + Telemetry** inherits
more than the plan assumed, because the quotation guard had to compute most of
the routing evidence to do its own job:

- **`QuotationAnalysis` already carries the article census** — `article_values`,
  `article_monotonic`, the quoted set, the omissis set and the citation
  antecedents. Plan §4's "article census, indentation discrimination, numbering
  monotonicity, omissis/citation cues" is largely computed; `routing/viability.py`
  can consume it rather than reimplement it, and **must not** become a second
  source of truth for the same measurements.
- **`DocSignals.rejected` is the telemetry seam.** Every candidate the inference
  refused is recorded with a human-readable reason (`orphan label '1.24.20.25 -'
  at block 2`, `top numeric series implausible: 1,11,111,46,194,74`). Plan
  invariant #10 wants exactly this, and `test_real_rejections_are_human_readable`
  already asserts every reason names a rule.
- **`DocSignals.coverage`** is computed per tree, which plan §4.2's coverage gate
  needs — but note it is computed *after* the annex split, as §4.2 requires,
  because the annex has its own tree.
- **The §4.4 route table is consistent with what was measured.** `port_mf_277` is
  the only sample whose article series is monotonic (`(1, 2)`); `parecer_93` and
  `par_cosit_26` are both non-monotonic, which is the discriminator §4.4's
  rationale column names for both.

Two cautions carried forward. First, `parecer_93`'s residual `IV`–`VI` chapters
mean the document is **not** flat, so any Cycle 4b rule keying off `tree.flat`
as a proxy for "unstructured" will misread it — route on the article census, not
on the tree's shape. Second, the referee's job on `par_cosit_26` (plan §2.6's

already convict all five of its quoted articles and bound the excerpt at the

rescue one that is not.
