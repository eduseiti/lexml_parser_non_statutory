## 1. Summary

**Verdict: complete.**

The nested `generico-aninhado` emitter is delivered. All 15 samples — 16
documents, counting `port_mf_277`'s annex — render as a real element tree, and
the 10 that have body sections to nest validate against **both**
`lexml-proposed/` schemas while being correctly *rejected* by the shipped ones,
which is what "opt-in" means. Text is identical to the flat emitter's, word for
word, across the whole bundle. Goldens are committed for all 16.

The cycle also discharges the **Cycle 0 addendum (A-R.2)**, which `STATUS.md`
has carried as *partial* since Cycle 4b landed the capability probe without the
matrix half. `MatrixCase` now carries `requires`, and a case skips with the
probe's own diagnostic rather than failing.

Two findings shaped the work, and neither was in the plan:

- **A-5b.1** — §5.4's Constraint 1 is broader than stated. Measured against
  `lexml-proposed/` before a line was written, a `Bloco` may not precede an
  `AgrupamentoHierarquico` either, so the order marker belongs *after* the
  subsections. An emitter written to the plan's literal wording emits invalid
  XML on any section that has both subsections and a marker.
- **A-5b.5** — six of the sixteen documents contain no `AgrupamentoHierarquico`
  at all. They are front and back matter, which both emitters render
  identically, so their nested output is byte-identical to their flat output
  and *correctly* valid on the shipped schemas.

### Emitted shape

```xml
<AgrupamentoHierarquico id="pp1_agh1" nome="secao">
  <Rotulo>2.</Rotulo>
  <NomeAgrupador>DAS SOCIEDADES COOPERATIVAS</NomeAgrupador>
  <AgrupamentoHierarquico id="pp1_agh1_agh1" nome="subsecao">
    <Rotulo>2.1 -</Rotulo>
    <NomeAgrupador>Empresas de serviços</NomeAgrupador>
    <Bloco nome="ordem">0</Bloco>
    <Agrupamento id="pp1_agh1_agh1_txt1" nome="texto"><p>Em linhas gerais…</p></Agrupamento>
  </AgrupamentoHierarquico>
  <Bloco nome="ordem">0</Bloco>
  <Agrupamento id="pp1_agh1_txt1" nome="texto"><p>…</p></Agrupamento>
</AgrupamentoHierarquico>
```

`§5.2`'s snippet writes the prose leaf as `pp1_agh1_txt`; delivered it is
`pp1_agh1_txt1`, because `IdAllocator.child()` always appends an ordinal. That is
the allocator's existing contract, unchanged, and the trailing `1` is what makes
a second prose leaf expressible at all.

### The measured content model

§5.4 says the constraints are "measured against `lexml-proposed/`, not assumed".
They were, in 24 probe cases, **before** the emitter was written:

```
AgrupamentoHierarquico[@id required][@nome required] ::=
    Rotulo?  NomeAgrupador?  AgrupamentoHierarquico*  (Agrupamento | Bloco | LXhierCompleto)+
```

Confirmed valid: subsections before own prose; `<Bloco nome="vazio"/>` as the
only non-`AH` child; `ordem` after the subsections; either native alone or
neither; four-deep nesting; tables and nested `ol`/`ul` inside a prose leaf;
flat `Agrupamento` as a *sibling* of an `AH` under `PartePrincipal` (which is
what lets A-5.1's regions coexist with a nested body).

Confirmed **invalid**: prose before subsections; a `Bloco` before a subsection
(**A-5b.1**, new); an `AH` with no non-`AH` child; a bare `<p>` under an `AH`
(so §2.1 row E survives the change, correctly); `NomeAgrupador` before `Rotulo`;
`AH` without `@id` or without `@nome`; an empty `<Agrupamento/>`; an `AH` nested
inside a flat `Agrupamento`.

## 7. Deviations and open items

1. **The prose leaf id is `…_txt1`, not `…_txt`** as §5.2's snippet writes it.
   `IdAllocator.child()` appends an ordinal unconditionally; that is its
   existing contract and the trailing `1` is what makes a second prose leaf
   expressible. Not worth a special case in the allocator for cosmetics.
2. **Constraint 1's cost is real and is only mitigated, not removed.** Sibling
   position no longer means reading order, and `ordem` is what recovers it. §11
   already carries the upstream refinement that would restore natural ordering;
   this cycle is evidence for it, not a substitute.
3. **`vazio` is exercised by exactly two samples**, `pn_cst_38` (5 occurrences)
   and `CARNE_LEAO` (1) — 6 in the corpus. Not dead code, but thinly covered by
   real documents; a synthetic fixture carries the rest of its weight.
4. **Six documents do not exercise nesting at all** (A-5b.5). For those, this
   emitter is the flat emitter, and cross-emitter equivalence is trivially true.
   The equivalence claim is carried by the other ten.
5. **The `.pyc` churn in `git status` is pre-existing.** 22 bytecode files were
   committed by cycles before Cycle 2 added `.gitignore`; removing them is
   explicitly deferred there and is not this cycle's business.
6. **A segment URN is not portable between emitters** (A-5b.4). Found by the
   cross-emitter subagent, which refused to weaken its test to hide it. Beyond
   the documented `agr`/`agh` token difference there is a **top-level ordinal
   offset**: the flat emitter numbers body sections in the same root sequence as
   the front regions, so `pn_cst_38`'s first section is `pp1_agr4` flat and
   `pp1_agh1` nested. I verified this independently. The offset is asserted as
   exactly the front-region count, so a third drift fails loudly. **Cycle 7
   should treat this as input**: a consumer holding `!pp1_agr4` cannot resolve it
   against nested output.
7. **The order of §2.10's `pn_cst_38` example is breadth-then-depth, not one
   chain.** `2.1` and `2.3` are siblings, so `2.` → `2.1` → `2.3` → `2.3.1` has
   ancestor depths `[0, 1, 1, 2]`. The genuinely four-deep chain is
   `6.` → `6.3` → `I` → `a)`. Both are asserted — a maximum nesting of three
   would otherwise pass a test whose whole subject is that arbitrary depth is
   now expressible. Reported by the emitter-test subagent and verified by me.
8. **Two traps recorded for future test authors**, both hit by the cross-emitter
   subagent in its own code: `startswith("agr")` also matches `agrf1` (back
   matter), so back matter silently joins a body comparison it happens to pass;
   and a document root is not one underscore token — the primary's is `pp1` but
   an annex's is `anexo1_pp`, so naive splitting excludes the annex, the one
   document where the emitters have most to disagree about.
9. **Nothing was left undone.** Every Cycle 5b bullet and test in the plan is
   discharged, and the Cycle 0 addendum it was asked to carry is discharged too.
